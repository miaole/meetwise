3. **Post-prove dual receipts BOTH PASS**（`mw-e2e-ha` + `mw-rag-route`）citing that committed prove tip · **evidence of record = dual** · implementer-only receipts **≠** evidence of record.
4. **Real PG + PostgresSaver path**（cite `adr-postgres-retained.md`）· **Ban** MemorySaver · **Ban** MySQL · **Ban** Qdrant-as-required · **Ban** stub/fake checkpointer as covered evidence.
